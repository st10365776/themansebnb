01 / THE MANSE

Welcome

A comfortable base in the heart of Qonce.

Before becoming a bed and breakfast, The Mansa was a house belonging to the Baptist Church in King William’s Town (now Qonce), Eastern Cape. The house was used as the residence for the Baptist Church’s minister and his family. One of the ministers who lived in the house was Reverend Jimmy Hendrix, who stayed there with his family during his time serving the church.

The property was traditionally known as “The Manse”, the same name by which the Baptist Church’s ministerial residence was known. It therefore has a history that goes beyond simply being a private residence or accommodation establishment — it formed part of the local Baptist Church community and was once a home connected to the church and its ministry.

Over the years, the property changed from its original use as the Baptist Church residence and eventually became a bed and breakfast, while retaining the historic name The Manse This gives the B&B a particularly interesting connection to the history of King William’s Town/Qonce and to the Baptist Church community that once used the property as its ministerial home.
